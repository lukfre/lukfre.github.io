Hi! :wave: My name is **Luca Gioffré** (`[lˈuka d͡ʒoffrˈe]`).

I'm a PhD Student in _Computational Linguistics_ at [SapienzaNLP](https://nlp.uniroma1.it/) in Sapienza, University of Rome where I am supervised by [Roberto Navigli](https://www.diag.uniroma1.it/navigli/).
My research focuses on LLM evaluation, long-form understanding and narrative reasoning.

I previously graduated in Sapienza twice, with a Bachelor degree in Engineering in Computer Science and Automatic Systems and a Master degree in Engineering in Computer Science.

I'm always keen to talk about my research and learning more about what other people are doing; feel free to reach out at my email!
