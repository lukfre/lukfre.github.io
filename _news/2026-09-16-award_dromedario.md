Very happy to share that our paper *Dromedario 3: Localizing the Tülu 3 Dataset into Italian* has received the **Best Student Paper Award** at [CLiC-it 2026](https://clic2026.unipa.it/), recognised as [an outstanding contribution to Italian NLP resources](https://clic2026.unipa.it/awards/).

Thank you to the award committee — Francesco Cutugno, Malvina Nissim and Roberto Zamparelli — for the recognition and for the care they clearly put into reading the work.

Dromedario 3 came out of a simple question: what would it take for Italian to have a post-training resource of the same quality as the English ones everyone builds on? The answer was a lot of patient localization work, and it's genuinely rewarding to see that effort recognised.
