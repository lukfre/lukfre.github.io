I'm proud to share that [Simone Laganà](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simone-lagan%C3%A0-486538243/), whom I had the pleasure of co-advising with my supervisor Roberto Navigli, has successfully defended his Master's thesis!

His thesis, **Teaching Language Models to Understand Italian Vernacular Varieties**, investigates how well large language models understand three Italian vernaculars: Roman, Neapolitan, and Sicilian.
To measure this, Simone introduced a new parallel benchmark pairing Italian with each variety.
His results show that models in the 7–9B parameter range perform noticeably worse on these varieties than on standard Italian. To close this gap, he built a new training dataset, and fine-tuning on it successfully improved the models' comprehension of these varieties.
The thesis also analyzes how these performance gaps relate to linguistic distance from Italian, tokenizer behavior, and the language composition of pre- and post-training corpora. It is complemented by a human evaluation study and a manual validation of the test set.

This work will soon form the basis of a new publication — stay tuned!

Congratulations, Simone! 👏
