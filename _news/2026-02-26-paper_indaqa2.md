## INDAQA2 - A Large Italian Narrative QA Benchmark: A CALAMITA 2026 Challenge

INDAQA2 is a manually validated resource designed to fill an important gap in the evaluation of narrative understanding for LLMs on Italian narrative texts. It spans over 460 books and includes more than 25k question-answering pairs. The benchmark features a dual evaluation procedure: a subset of open-ended questions is also presented in multiple-choice format, enabling a more reliable assessment of structural biases introduced by evaluation and prompting formats.
