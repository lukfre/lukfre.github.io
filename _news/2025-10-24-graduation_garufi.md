Very happy to share that [Giacomo Garufi](https://it.linkedin.com/in/giacomo-garufi-96553b2a6), whom I had the pleasure of co-advising alongside my supervisor Roberto Navigli, has successfully completed his Master's thesis!

His thesis, **LIRA-100: An analysis of Italian Long-context Narrative Understanding**, explores how large language models handle long narrative texts in Italian, introducing a new benchmark to evaluate their comprehension and reasoning abilities.

The work is exciting and will soon be the foundation of [a new publication]({{ site.baseurl }}/news/2026-02-26-paper_indaqa2) — stay tuned!

Congratulations, Giacomo! 👏
