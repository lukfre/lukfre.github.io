Bio

Luca Gioffré is a third-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer, Control, and Managemenet Engineering at Sapienza University of Rome, where he is a member of the Sapienza NLP Lab, under the supervision of Prof. Roberto Navigli.
He has authored several publications in top-tier NLP conferences, including ACL and EMNLP, as well as at CLiC-it, the Italian Conference on Computational Linguistics.
His research centers on reading comprehension over long-form narrative documents, first through evaluation and meta-evaluation, and now on improving how models extract information and reason over long, stylistically difficult texts via targeted training methods.
He also contributes to the Minerva LLM family, where he works on post-training and evaluation.
